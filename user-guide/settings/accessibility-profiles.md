# ♿ Accessibility profiles

Accessibility Tools comes with its own predefined set of tools to ensure compliance and accessibility to a large variety of special needs users.&#x20;

To access this feature, from the left-hand side navigation pane, go to ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png>)_**Settings > Accessibility**_. In the **Accessibility** section, you can:

* Select a specific **Accessibility profile** depending on your specific needs:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Adjust the content throughout the app based on certain variables by using the sliders or by selecting any existing profiles that might match what you are looking for:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Adjust the color scheme of Accessibility Tools - either manually (using the sliders) or by selecting specific profiles available to you:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Activate or deactivate navigation tools, such as reading mask and reading guide:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
