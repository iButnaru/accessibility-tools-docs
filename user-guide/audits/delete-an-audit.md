# 🗑️ Delete an audit

To delete an audit:

1. On the left-hand side navigation pane, click ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (141).png>) **Audits**.
2. Identify and select the audit that you want to **delete**.
3. Click the ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (96).png>) **Settings** button next to the desired audit.&#x20;
4. On the shortcut menu, click **Delete audit**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (144).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. In the **Delete audit** screen, tick the _"Yes, I'm sure"_ box, then click **Delete**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (145).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
