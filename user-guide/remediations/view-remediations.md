# 👀 View remediations

To access remediations, click ![](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXe2sNK7gdNDhoaSpJURhZv0s9jAsOFANn7utwUKTqDcBY63OhjHliRusxTRmzNd9i9cF_N2ohfZgqBA7q21yoIjLNbAi7mUzbN8bsITo5WqGdas4sBfxs6wQXqUbpJruh05lJE4?key=_EQKB1eDe9ecbVZeBDg4Tw) **Remediations** on the left-hand side navigation pane. All available remediations are displayed, together with some of the details, such as code, category, target and so on.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (121).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To filter the remediations list, click ![](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfHvr7qDbO-rzXlZqZE1fAx23ajf-MNFiBLVneX3e8AUh0y5aCiahEn8mxwwxPX6Y5QxD-3KhfhfGkaqv2Fbirnc7J2tvTe9bbpVPITa3o7cAbCE5oFCybnOIciuiGZL0JkvdlVzw?key=_EQKB1eDe9ecbVZeBDg4Tw)**Filter by** menu and select the desired filtering condition:&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (122).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After selecting the desired filters, click the ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (123).png>) **Apply filters** button to save.&#x20;
